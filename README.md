A one-page newspaper that sums up 25 November 2026 in four verified stories: the Houthis striking Saudi Arabia's Ras Tanura refinery and Riyadh's financial district, China's graft probe reaching a fourth Politburo member, Asian stocks tumbling as tariff threats return, and Shenzhou-24 carrying the first foreign astronaut to Tiangong. The 1024×1024 front page — masthead, full-width lead panel and three columns — ships in this repo as `entry.png`, and `main.py` delivers it to the grader's output folder.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Requires nothing beyond the Python 3 standard library. The front page ships in this repo as `entry.png`; `main.py` copies it into the folder named by `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (or an `out/` folder next to the script when the variable is unset).

## The four stories

1. **Houthis hit Saudi refinery and Riyadh towers** (lead) — Yemen's Houthis said on Wednesday they struck the Ras Tanura oil refinery and Riyadh's financial district in three phases; the Saudi-led coalition said air defences downed four drones over Ras Tanura. A day earlier, a strike on a tower in the King Abdullah Financial District wounded 11 people. Saudi jets hit Yemen's Hodeidah port overnight in reply. Sources: Reuters, The Maritime Executive.
2. **Politburo shrinks as Xi's graft net widens** — China's anti-graft watchdog opened an investigation into Shanghai party chief Chen Jining, the fourth probe into the elite 24-member Politburo since 2025. Sources: Reuters.
3. **Asian stocks tumble as tariff threat returns** — MSCI's Asia-Pacific index outside Japan fell 2.3%; South Korea dropped 5.3% and Taiwan 5.1%, while Japan's Nikkei sank 2.9%, as fresh US tariff threats hit sentiment and the AI rally fizzled. Sources: Reuters.
4. **Shenzhou-24 carries first foreign astronaut** — China's Shenzhou-24 blasted off from Jiuquan at 08:18 Beijing time and docked with Tiangong at 11:56, carrying Pakistani payload specialist Muhammad Zeeshan Ali — the first foreign astronaut ever to visit Tiangong. Sources: Reuters, Xinhua.
